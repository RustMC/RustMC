# ADR-0003: Single-process deployment and minimal workspace

Status: **Proposed** (29 September 2026).

## Context and constraints
M0 needs a small buildable foundation without speculative subsystems. M0 has no gameplay implementation; compatibility and performance claims need evidence.

## Options considered
One process and one crate; multiple processes or pre-split crates.

## Decision
Begin with one rustmc-server package and proposed single-machine process.

## Consequences and risks
Simple setup; later decomposition may be necessary.

## Tests and replacement criteria
Replace after measured scaling, fault, or maintainability pressure.
