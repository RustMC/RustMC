# ADR-0005: Pulse–Parcel experiment

Status: **Experimental** (29 September 2026).

## Context and constraints
Partitioning may change same-tick reads, order, randomness, migration, barriers, and storage pressure. M0 has no gameplay implementation; compatibility and performance claims need evidence.

## Options considered
Always serial; partitioned execution with semantic coordination.

## Decision
Experiment only after reference behavior exists; no scheduler in M0.

## Consequences and risks
Potential concurrency gain is unproven and coordination may erase it.

## Tests and replacement criteria
Reject on same-tick or partition replay divergence, duplicate/lost migration actions, unsafe persistence, or no realistic measured benefit.
