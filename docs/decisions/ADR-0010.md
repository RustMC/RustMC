# ADR-0010: Coordinate-derived terrain prototype

Status: **Accepted for the first original terrain slice only** (29 September 2026).

## Context
A future client join needs a small, repeatable world without importing any server generator, game asset, or undocumented vanilla parity claim. Chunk requests may arrive in any order. Decorations crossing chunk edges must have a single answer for each world coordinate.

## Decision
Generate terrain from a caller-provided 64-bit seed and integer world coordinates. Two scales of interpolated integer lattice values set the surface height. Fixed ground layers provide bedrock, stone, dirt, and grass. A second coordinate-derived field creates tree clearings. Each 8-by-8 world cell may place one tree at a deterministic offset; a chunk considers roots just outside its borders and clips the resulting trunk and leaves to its own cells. The generated chunk has a fixed 16-by-16-by-128 block volume. The generator has no mutable random stream, disk state, or request-order dependency.

## Consequences and tests
This is original illustrative terrain, not vanilla generation, biome parity, or a playable world. Tests check seed stability, different seeds, negative coordinates, layers, request-order independence, and tree canopy crossing a chunk edge. Future chunk encoding must map these internal block types through a separately sourced version-specific registry. Generation, encoding, and network delivery timings must be reported separately.
